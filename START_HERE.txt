# BASE — Your App System

This is your creator app system.

To use it:

1. Open the Creator Dashboard
   → go to: /creator-Dashboard/index.html

2. Enter your Creator Key

3. Connect your content.json URL

4. Click "Load Current State"

5. Edit and Publish your app

The Fan App will not display correctly if opened directly from this folder.

It must be published (GitHub Pages or hosting) to fully work.
